★ THE FORMAT

24 TEAMS.
6 GROUPS.
ONE TROPHY.

Euro 2028 keeps the 24-team format that's defined the Euros since 2016 — six groups, a knockout bracket with room for upsets, and a month of football across the four nations of the UK and the Republic of Ireland.

  • 6 groups of 4. Top two from each group advance, plus the four best third-placed teams — 16 teams reach the knockouts.
  • 51 matches in 31 days. From group-stage chaos to a Round of 16 that's always one penalty shootout away from a national crisis.
  • Five host nations, one tournament. England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and Republic of Ireland share hosting — UEFA has reserved up to two qualifying lifelines for hosts that miss out on the regular path.
  • The final at Wembley. Where football coming home is no longer a meme — it's the literal venue.
