★ THE FORMAT

32 TEAMS.
8 GROUPS.
ONE TROPHY.

The 2027 Women's World Cup keeps the 32-team format that defined Australia & New Zealand 2023 — eight groups, a 16-team knockout, and 64 matches across 32 days. First time the tournament lands in South America.

  • 8 groups of 4. Top two from each group advance to a straight Round of 16 — no third-placed lifelines.
  • 64 matches in 32 days. Group stage chaos, then a knockout bracket where one mistake sends you home.
  • Single host, one country. First single-nation Women's World Cup since France 2019 — no border-hopping, just Brazil.
  • Holders return. Spain arrive as defending champions after their 1–0 win over England in Sydney in 2023.
