The 2026 World Cup is the first ever with 48 nations — the largest tournament in the sport's history. More matches, more upsets, more places to lose your bet.

  • 12 groups of 4. Top two from each group advance, plus the eight best third-placed teams.
  • 32-team knockout. A new Round of 32 kicks off the bracket — one slip-up and you're out.
  • 104 matches in 39 days. Up from 64 in Qatar 2022. Plenty for your Betty group to chew on.
  • Three host nations. USA hosts 78 matches, Mexico and Canada split the rest. The final is in New Jersey.
