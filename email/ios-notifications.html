Quick heads-up — Betty for iPhone now supports push notifications. Kick-off reminders, late goals, and the group chat all ping you the moment they happen, straight from the app.

If you said “not now” the first time iOS asked — or just never got the prompt — the switch is still off. Worth a quick check in your phone’s Settings so you don’t miss the good stuff.

 

★  Two taps

HOW TO FLIP
THE SWITCH.

01

Open Settings. On your iPhone, scroll to Betty.

02

Tap Notifications. Toggle “Allow Notifications” on.
